# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha — unreleased

The custom ring surface of `@heliuslabs/zolana` 0.4.0-alpha moves into `@heliuslabs/zolana-custom-rings`, which takes `@heliuslabs/spr` as a pinned peer, and `CustomRingClient` adds the ring proofs and ring indexer reads to an `SprClient`. A ring holds its rules and role keys in one `RingPolicy` account, its lists and mint settings in Storage Program records and each owner's nullifier key in a tracking registry, and it runs in the `confidential` or the `anonymous` mode. Every ring operation sends as one transaction whose auditor stream opens under its transaction viewing key.

Breaking

- Zero-key transfer recipients must be absent at the selected tracking root. An enrolled recipient must receive under its registered nullifier key.
- `AnonymousTransfer.prepareRelay` sends the `withOwnerExposure` choice to Ring RPC, and Ring RPC refuses a hidden owner that funds a leg, receives an exit or spends a zero-key note unless the choice is `"accepted"` → run Ring RPC of the same revision.

- `DelegateTransfer` with an `exit` output fails before any proof with `RING_EXIT_PROOF_UNAVAILABLE` as the code or the cause code → pay delegate moves inside the ring or out through a public `withdrawal` until a release proves the exit.

Changed

- `AnonymousTransfer` refuses a hidden owner as payer, funder or exit destination and its zero-key spends unless `withOwnerExposure("accepted")` admits them, refuses a hidden co-signer with `RING_CO_SIGNER_INVALID` in every case, and lets exits pay several destinations.
- A ring transfer pays an output key its owner never registered, `resolveRingRecipient` resolves such an address as `unregistered` under its own keys in place of raising `RING_RECIPIENT_NOT_ENROLLED`, a transfer refuses a literal zero key with `RING_RECIPIENT_LITERAL_ZERO_KEY`, and `delegateNoteKey` opens each delegate note under the key it commits.
- `AnonymousScan.sent` lists a payment only under the recipient key whose slot tag the published tag matches, and change opens by trial under the wallet's viewing key.

Fixed

- A spend of a zero-key or co-owner note on an `SprClient` whose prover reads the proof data, the default, raised `CLIENT_INVALID_PROOF_AUTHORITY`, now the ring spend authority also proves through `proveIndexed` and fills those slots there.
- A confidential withdrawal of an SPL asset on a ring whose policy reads withdrawals raised `RING_ANONYMOUS_EXIT_TOKEN_ACCOUNT_INVALID` while the recipient had no token account, now the withdrawal builders name the recipient as its owner and `CustomRingTransfer.withWithdrawalAccounts` takes that owner.
- A relayed spend on a ring reading a salted list left the sender's list salt out of the relay payload, and Ring RPC refused it with `transferRefused`, now the payload carries the sender's salt with the other owners'.
- `RingRpc.getDecryptedTransactions` refused a transaction whose slot kept its record in `outputs` beside a `payload` error, as Ring RPC serves it, now a slot may sit once in each list and a slot twice in one list stays refused.
- A ring entry from a default note larger than its amount asked approval for the amount alone while the change also entered the ring, now the `ringEntry` intent and its summary name every unit entering the ring.
- `PreparedRelay.send` called again after a lost answer asked Ring RPC to prepare the relay a second time, now it reads the relay's status first, returns the relay submitted once Ring RPC sent it and raises `RING_RELAY_FAILED` when Ring RPC failed it.
- `recoverRingMemberNotes` failed on one output whose mint no registry names, now that commitment stays in `unopened` and recovery goes on.
- `recordChain` and `ringTransferStatement` hashed a record of any length into a statement the program never verifies, now a record off both published lengths raises `RING_INVALID_LENGTH` before any proof.
